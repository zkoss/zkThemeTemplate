#### 4.2 Scope growth — **evidence of self-sufficiency**, with one caveat (mixed)

The growth was not unilateral. The drill proposed the contract expansion as decision **D1** and
waited; the user ruled `D1:a` at 14:13. D4/D5 similarly. The brief's own escape clause ("if a
